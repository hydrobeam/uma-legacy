The model is the loop planner's (How this works), with what your veterans really carry: each legacy's stars per skill, her recorded G1 wins and her parents. A legacy without a spark of a skill passes nothing on, and the new veteran's spark gets 10% more likely per ancestor that has it. Aptitude (pink) sparks don't change the skill odds; the page shows how likely each aptitude is passed on.
