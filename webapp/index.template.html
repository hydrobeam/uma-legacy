Model. Each target spark sits on all six legacies with stars from the chosen rank: 50/45/5% for 1/2/3 stars below rank score 17,500 and 20/70/10% from 17,500 up (room-match data); a spark's base rate is 3/6/9% by stars. A skill is acquired if guaranteed, on the trainee's own outfit skill list (fully awakened), hinted by the deck, or inherited: proc = 1 − Π(1 − base × (1 + affinity/100)) over the six legacies, two inspirations. Each legacy's affinity follows uma-it-optimizer's fit to 8,155 Independent Training receipts: a parent counts its relation points with the trainee, the full relation with the other parent, its trios with the trainee and its own parents, and 3 per G1 shared with the other parent and with each of its own parents; a grandparent counts its trio with the trainee and its child, plus 3 per G1 shared with that child. It then becomes a spark 35.4% of the time as a white, 70.9% as a gold: 20% and 40%, ×1.1 for each of the six ancestors already carrying it (fit to room-match data). Odds average the 12 runs of the self-copy-free cycle.

Deck. Six cards, every card at max limit break. Training hints: 1 + hint frequency/25 per run, each a uniform draw over the card's hint options. Chain events: a Poisson(7.1) number per run, each advancing a random remaining chain slot of a non-pal card (fits 81% / 59% / 50% full-chain completion with 6 / 8 / 9 slots); pals add no chain slots. Event rewards: GameTora training-event data; a reward on only one outcome counts at 50%. Pal finales (Light Hello SSR See Ya Later!, Anshinzawa SSR Nothing Ventured) are certain; first-year date events are left out (they don't fire in independent training). The deck and the loop are searched together and the result is exact for this model.

Search. Every foursome gets a guaranteed ceiling from exact pairwise bounds; loops are scheduled best-first until no remaining ceiling can beat the 5th result. Schedules maximise the expected shared G1 wins, every pair weighted alike (each pair carries the same weight across the 12 careers). Win odds per race: 110 − distance cost − surface cost (A 0, B 10, C 20, D 30, E 40, F 60, G 90), minus 0/0/5/20/30/50 for the 1st to 6th+ race in a row, capped at 0–100%. Data: Global master.mdb, relocated venues count as the same race. The Grand Masters-era option uses a JP master.mdb cut at L'Arc's launch (24 Aug 2023), with group cards counted as pals.
