Portraits and card art

The page ships without game art and shows coloured initials and card-type badges instead. To see real portraits and card images, add your own image files here. They stay in this browser only and are never uploaded.

Name each file by its game id: 4 digits for an uma (1001.png is Special Week), 6 digits for an outfit (100102.png is Special Week [Hopp'n♪Happy Heart]), 5 digits for a support card (30116.png is Daitaku Helios SSR [Sunlit Angel ♪]). Any image type works; extra words in the name are ignored as long as the id is the only number.

Hosting the page yourself

Model. Each target spark sits on all six legacies with stars from the chosen rank. A skill is acquired if guaranteed, on the trainee's own outfit skill list (fully awakened), hinted by the deck, or inherited: proc = 1 − Π(1 − base × (1 + affinity/100)), grandparents at half rate, two inspirations. It then becomes a spark 35% of the time as a white, 70% as a gold. Odds average the 12 runs of the self-copy-free cycle.

Deck. Six cards, every card at max limit break. Training hints: 1 + hint frequency/25 per run, each a uniform draw over the card's hint options. Chain events: a Poisson(7.1) number per run, each advancing a random remaining chain slot of a non-pal card (fits 81% / 59% / 50% full-chain completion with 6 / 8 / 9 slots); pals add no chain slots. Event rewards: GameTora training-event data; a reward on only one outcome counts at 50%. Pal finales (Light Hello SSR See Ya Later!, Anshinzawa SSR Nothing Ventured) are certain; first-year date events are left out (they don't fire in independent training). The deck and the loop are searched together and the result is exact for this model.

Search. Every foursome gets a guaranteed ceiling from exact pairwise bounds; loops are scheduled best-first until no remaining ceiling can beat the 5th result. Schedules maximise affinity with grandparent slots at half weight. Win odds per race: 110 − distance cost − surface cost (A 0, B 10, C 20, D 30, E 40, F 60, G 90), minus 0/0/5/20/30/50 for the 1st to 6th+ race in a row, capped at 0–100%. Data: Global master.mdb, relocated venues count as the same race. The Grand Masters-era option uses a JP master.mdb cut at L'Arc's launch (24 Aug 2023), with group cards counted as pals.
